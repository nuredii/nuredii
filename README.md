## Hi, I'm Nur Üredi 👋

I'm a PhD student in Midwifery at Ege University (İzmir, Türkiye). My work looks at how AI-assisted and simulation-based tools can support midwifery education and women's health, and how well they actually perform.

🇹🇷 Ege Üniversitesi Sağlık Bilimleri Enstitüsü'nde Ebelik doktora öğrencisiyim. Yapay zekâ destekli ve simülasyon tabanlı araçların ebelik eğitimi ile kadın sağlığında nasıl kullanılabileceğini ve bu araçların ne kadar etkili olduğunu araştırıyorum.

---

### 🔬 Research interests

- AI chatbots in health education: RAG-based chatbots, adolescent sexual and reproductive health
- Evaluating large language models: accuracy of AI-generated answers on pregnancy, childbirth and postpartum care
- Simulation-based clinical education: CTG interpretation, breastfeeding assessment, labour monitoring
- Research methodology: systematic reviews, meta-analysis, quasi-experimental designs

### 🌐 Live tools

| Tool | Description |
|---|---|
| [**Gestational Age & Due Date Calculator**](https://nuredii.github.io/gebelik-haftasi-hesaplayici/) | Turkish-language learning tool for midwifery students: due date by LMP (280-day rule vs. Naegele's rule), gestational age and trimester, with 10 step-by-step practice cases. Based on ACOG Committee Opinion No. 700. ([source code](https://github.com/nuredii/gebelik-haftasi-hesaplayici)) |

### 🛠️ Projects in progress

| Project | Description | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **FetaLab** | CTG/NST interpretation training game: students classify fetal heart rate traces and get instant feedback | Prototype |
| **LATCH Simulator** | Web-based breastfeeding assessment simulator with case animations, student scoring and expert–student agreement (Cohen's κ) | In development |
| **Labour Care Guide Simulator** | Training simulator based on the WHO Labour Care Guide (next-generation partograph) | Early stage |

*These are educational tools and are not intended for clinical decision-making.*
